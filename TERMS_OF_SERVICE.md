*Last updated: November 2026*

By installing or using the **Automated Climate Action Maker (ACAM)** browser extension, you agree to these terms.

---

## 1. What ACAM Does

ACAM is a free, open-source browser extension that analyzes your browsing patterns locally on your device and suggests climate-positive actions such as signing petitions, finding refurbished alternatives, and making lower-emission food choices. All processing happens on your machine — ACAM never transmits your data to external servers.

## 2. No Account Required

ACAM does not require registration, login, or any personal information to function.

## 3. Your Data Stays Local

All browsing analysis, recommendations, and point tracking are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. You can purge all stored data at any time from the extension popup. See our [Privacy Policy](https://krzheng3150.github.io/automated-climate-action-maker/privacy.html) for full details.

## 4. External Links

When you click an action button (e.g., signing a petition or browsing a refurbished marketplace), ACAM opens that website in a new tab. Your interaction with that external website is governed by its own terms and privacy policy, not ours.

## 5. Voluntary Feedback

If you choose to share feedback via the Share Feedback button, that action is user-initiated and opens an external page. No feedback is collected automatically.

## 6. No Warranty
