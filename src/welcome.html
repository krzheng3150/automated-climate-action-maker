Welcome to ACAM

Climate-positive nudges, powered entirely by your browser.

🔍

Detects context

Recognizes when you're on a shopping product page or reading climate news. No browsing data is recorded or stored.

🧠

Analyzes on your device

All classification happens locally in your browser's memory. Nothing is sent to any server — not ours, not anyone's.

💡

Suggests alternatives

Shows refurbished options on product pages and civic actions on climate articles. You decide what to do.

No network requests. Ever.
🔒

Zero network calls

The extension makes no HTTP requests. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no server calls.

🗑️

No URL storage

Full URLs are never saved. Only domain-level visit counts are kept locally, and you can purge them anytime.

📦

Everything is local

Classification data, action suggestions, and your settings all live in your browser's local storage.

👁️

Fully auditable

Open the extension popup and check System Diagnostics to see every decision ACAM makes in real time.

ACAM is open source. Every line of code is inspectable.
